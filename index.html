Free Online Math Quiz

This free online math quiz helps students and learners practice arithmetic skills through quick, timed challenges. Each round consists of 10 randomly generated questions covering the four basic operations.

How to Practice Math With This Quiz

Start a quiz, read the equation carefully, and type your answer before the 10-second timer expires. You receive immediate feedback after every question. At the end you see your total score and percentage so you can track improvement over time. The quiz continues through all 10 questions even if you answer incorrectly, giving you a complete practice session every time.

Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication and Division

Questions use numbers from 1 to 40. Addition, subtraction, and multiplication expect whole-number answers. Division answers are calculated to two decimal places. The game never divides by zero. Regular practice with mixed operations builds speed, accuracy, and confidence in mental math.

Whether you are a student preparing for school, a parent looking for a simple practice tool, or anyone wanting a quick brain workout, this math quiz offers a clean, focused experience with no distractions and no sign-up required.
